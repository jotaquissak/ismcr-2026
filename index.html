About the Symposium

ISMCR 2026 is the 27th edition of the International Symposium on Measurement and Control in Robotics, organized by IMEKO TC17 — the Technical Committee on Measurement in Robotics. IMEKO, the International Measurement Confederation, was founded in 1958 and is a non-governmental federation of 42 member organizations, with consultative status at UNESCO and UNIDO, dedicated to advancing measurement science and technology.

The ISMCR series began in 1988 at the NASA Johnson Space Center in Houston, USA, and has since travelled the world, gathering researchers and engineers to share advances in measurement, control and robotics. Now held annually, ISMCR is one of the many technical symposia promoted by IMEKO's Technical Committees, which each organize their own events in their fields of expertise. IMEKO also holds its World Congress every three years — the next edition will take place in Rimini, Italy, in 2027.

As an international federation, IMEKO unites the national metrology institutes and measurement societies of more than 40 countries, creating a global network that strengthens the exchange of knowledge and the comparability of measurement results around the world. In Brazil, this role is held by INMETRO — the National Institute of Metrology, Quality and Technology — the federal body responsible for scientific, industrial and legal metrology, for accrediting laboratories and for ensuring the reliability of the measurements that support industry, health, safety and trade. INMETRO's endorsement of ISMCR 2026 reinforces the scientific relevance of the symposium, connecting its discussions on measurement, control and robotics to the credibility of the Brazilian metrology system.

ISMCR 2026 is organized by Universidade Estácio de Sá, one of the largest private higher education institutions in Brazil. Founded in Rio de Janeiro in 1970, Estácio grew from a single law school into a national educational network, with more than 50 campuses and over 2,000 distance-learning centers distributed throughout the country. Today it is part of YDUQS, the largest private higher education group in Brazil, which serves more than 1.3 million students across campuses and distance-learning centers. Internationalization is central to Estácio's mission: by hosting events such as ISMCR, the university strengthens the connection between Brazilian research and the global academic community.

The symposium will take place at the IDOMED auditorium, in Barra da Tijuca, at Città América shopping mall. IDOMED — Instituto de Educação Médica — is the medical education brand of the YDUQS group, born from the union of Estácio's medical schools and carrying more than 20 years of tradition in training physicians across Brazil. The event also counts on the support of Instituto Yduqs, the social arm of the group, whose mission is to expand access to education and promote sustainable social transformation through high-impact projects in education, social development, health and the environment.

The symposium also counts on the support of two of Estácio's partners — Algetec, which provides the virtual laboratories used across our courses, and Promove, which supports the production of our events.
